# Visible Motion Review

Name the action the viewer is supposed to perceive. Inspect time points that cover its beginning, change and result at delivery size. Check the subject's motion or deformation itself; camera movement, captions and particles cannot establish a missing central action.

Separate factual/script correctness, asset attribution, subject action, edit/audio timing, technical export and audience response. A rendered file is evidence of rendering only. When useful, collect contact sheets or a small geometric measurement that makes the visible change testable.

After feedback, fix the relevant action/shot or layer and preserve the already accepted parts. Do not claim audience satisfaction or publication from an export result or a caption document.
