# Evidence Corpus Pipeline

Keep originals unchanged. Assign stable source/version IDs and maintain locators from derived text to source page, paragraph or block. Distinguish unavailable, partial, archived, parsed, read, analyzed and reviewed material; report the dimensions relevant to this job instead of one ambiguous completion percentage.

Use deterministic enumeration, extraction, deduplication and basic metadata processing for broad coverage. Concentrate deep model reading and independent review on the actual question, close neighbors, important conclusions, disputed classification and questionable source quality. Do not pre-read an entire corpus at the same costly depth merely because future questions may exist.

Choose real stratified quality samples. Preserve review cases instead of turning them into PASS to hit a quota. Derived design patterns or summaries are analyst interpretation, not original-author statements. Successful extraction does not establish originality or semantic correctness.
